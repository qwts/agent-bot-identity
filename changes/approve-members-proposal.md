- The local web UI's Agent machine section lists the member accounts plans saved by `managed-machine members plan`, and **Review** opens a proposal in the Approvals tab showing every step and the exact content of the tmpfiles entry and sudoers rule. Approving sends agent-machine the signed `approve-members` request (`plan_id` and `plan_digest` only), which records the approval for 10 minutes and runs nothing; the owner then runs `sudo managed-machine members apply <plan-id>`. The daemon recomputes the plan digest and refuses a saved plan that does not match it, and `POST /v0/machine` refuses the verb with `machine-verb-owner-only`, so no agent binding can send it (#1401).
