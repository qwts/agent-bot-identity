- A daemon launch probes the harness sign-in at a `sign-in` stage before the soul joins, with the exact environment the harness turn gets (#536). A positive sign-out of an existing soul whose store is not routed fails the launch with `harness-signed-out` and the `agent-bot harness auth login` command. A new soul, a routed store or an `unknown` probe continues, and the evidence is kept in the launch journal. The turn-environment composition is now shared (`composeTurnEnv`), so the probe and the harness turn cannot diverge.
