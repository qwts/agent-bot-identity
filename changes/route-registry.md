- The daemon's routes are registered through one route registry. Each route source (identity, soul, and the daemon's own groups) declares its routes with the auth class of each, as `docs/daemon-api.md` lists them, and the daemon's bearer check and soul-binding refusal read those classes. Routes, classes, responses and receipts are unchanged. It is the in-place form of HQ plugin registration (HQ plan §4.2, Stage 0 row 8, #1392).
