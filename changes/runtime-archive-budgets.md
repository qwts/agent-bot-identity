- Runtime and harness archives now have resource budgets. A download larger than 1 GiB is refused, by its stated length or as it streams, and leaves no partial file. Before `tar` runs, the archive is read in process and must be a gzip tar, ustar tar or zip; anything else is refused, including old tars without the ustar header, zips with bytes before them, xz, bzip2, zstd, cpio and 7z. It is also refused if it has more than 200,000 entries, expands past 4 GiB, nests deeper than 64 directories, holds a sparse file, device or FIFO, has a link or directory that carries data, or has a zip directory that disagrees with its end record. The catalog's archives are a small fraction of these limits. A digest pins an archive's bytes, not its size (#1072).
