- Runtime and harness archives now have resource budgets. A download larger than 1 GiB is refused, by its stated length or as it streams, and leaves no partial file. Before `tar` runs, a gzip tar, plain tar or zip archive is read in process and refused if it has more than 200,000 entries, expands past 4 GiB, nests deeper than 64 directories, or holds a sparse file, device or FIFO. The catalog's archives are a small fraction of these limits. A digest pins an archive's bytes, not its size (#1072).
